Publish an official response (right of reply)

Only publish responses whose sender you have confirmed — for example an email from an official address. The text is published unchanged next to the report and appears in its evidence trail. It does not change the report's status.

Check repeat reports

The app joins a new report to an earlier one when both are the same kind of problem within 40 m. Machines make mistakes, so please check each pair. ✓ Same: keep them joined. ✕ Not the same: the new report becomes its own report again. 🗑 Delete: remove the repeat photo from the site. Every choice shows on the report's public history. To join two reports the app missed, open one in "Find a report".

Cleanup drives

A drive shows as one banner on the report map from when you save it until a week after it starts. Its card lists the unresolved reports within 80 m of the route, then the ones fixed since the start, with before/after photos. Use only details the organisers published. Draw the route: tap the start, then each turn, then the end; "Follow roads" snaps each leg to walkable roads.
