04 / BEFORE YOU LEAVE

A good guest comes prepared.

Your departure checklist

Saved on this browser. Tick these after confirming.

Travel & access

Confirm your arrival station and pickup point. Ask your host about current road conditions and site restrictions. Keep a return transport plan. Map results do not establish that a place is open.

Waterfalls, heat & rain

Follow posted barriers and local authority instructions. Do not enter fast-flowing water or restricted areas. In rain or high heat, choose a shorter plan and confirm conditions before departure. Carry drinking water without drawing on scarce village supplies.

Culture & local livelihoods

Ask before entering homes, photographing people or attending community ceremonies. Workshops and performances require consent and agreed payment. Buy directly from artisans and keep a receipt where available.

Waste & facilities

Use a toilet stop confirmed by your provider. Carry a reusable bottle and a rubbish bag; take waste to an arranged collection point. A bin without collection is not a waste solution.

If something goes wrong

For immediate danger, use your phone's emergency calling feature or seek local police assistance. This site is not monitored for emergencies. For a service dispute, keep receipts and contact the provider. Civic reports below are local drafts until separately submitted.

06 / FROM WEBSITE TO WORKING SERVICE

The operator's playbook.

01 / VERIFY

Before listing

Inspect the service. Record evidence, inspector, consent and date. Confirm prices, capacity and cancellation policy. Never label a listing verified from a self-declaration.

02 / CONFIRM

Before each trip

Confirm access, host, vehicle, toilets and weather. Assign a trip coordinator and backup driver. Send a written quote and obtain explicit acceptance.

03 / CLOSE THE LOOP

After each trip

Record punctuality, actual costs, local payments and customer feedback. Assign complaints to an owner and close only after checking the outcome.

Service rules & launch requirements

Ready to use: destination discovery, external maps, itinerary generation, editable requests, printable plans, browser checklist and local issue drafts.

Before accepting bookings: recruit providers, verify services, establish an accountable operator, publish actual quotes and refund terms, and connect a secure booking and payment backend. This edition has no live inventory, dispatch, payments, public issue inbox or provider verification.

Planning rules: keep destinations in one circuit; exclude steep stops for gentle mobility; exclude waterfalls in rain; prefer short shaded activities in heat. Day three is a flexible day rather than a forced extra journey. Route order is a suggestion, not road-network optimisation.

Expansion rule: first complete 10 paid group trips, with positive contribution after direct costs and 9/10 timely pickups. Record local provider payments. Add a circuit only after that threshold.
