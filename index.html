The Geographic Truth

The map has been read upside down.

Purulia is not peripheral. It sits at the exact centre of a quadrilateral — Kolkata, Ranchi, Dhanbad, Bhubaneswar — four cities within 300 km. No other district in eastern India has this geometry.

The problem has never been location. It has been the absence of infrastructure to activate it. That is a solvable problem — and this blueprint solves it.

  • ☀️Highest solar irradiance in eastern India. 5.4 kWh/m²/day — 300 sunny days per year on flat laterite land that grows nothing. The same geography that makes farming impossible makes it India's best solar site east of Gujarat. A 1,200 MW solar-pumped storage project is already proposed.
  • 🎭UNESCO-listed, criminally undervalued. Chhau dance. 4,000-year-old Dokra metalwork. ~250 artisans in Charida village earn ₹2,000–3,000 per month at peak season making masks that have a GI tag and UNESCO recognition. The infrastructure doesn't match the heritage.
  • 🌿47% of tribal household income comes from forests — firewood, sal leaves, sabai grass. Mahua flowers worth ₹40/kg raw become ₹400/kg processed. The margin leaves to Kolkata. The forest is the economy — and it is being degraded.
  • 💧Drought is engineering, not fate. 66% of the district is under severe-to-extreme drought — yet the district receives 1,200mm of rain annually, 80% in 90 days. The fix is storage. Jharkhand built 5,000 check dams under MGNREGS. Purulia can replicate it block by block.
