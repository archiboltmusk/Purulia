The Geographic Truth

The map has been read upside down.

Purulia sits in the western corner of West Bengal, adjacent to Jharkhand's industrial belt and within reach of three state capitals. The problem has never been location. It has been the absence of infrastructure to activate it.

  • 💧Drought is engineering, not fate. Purulia is officially drought-prone, yet receives well over 1,000 mm of rain per year, mostly in the monsoon, on hard-rock ground that stores little of it. The fix is storage — check dams, farm ponds, restored pukurs — block by block.
  • 🎭World heritage, village incomes. Chhau dance has been on UNESCO's Intangible Cultural Heritage list since 2010. Charida's mask-makers and the district's Dokra metalworkers are known far beyond Purulia.
  • 🌿The forest is the economy. Forest produce — firewood, sal leaves, sabai grass, mahua — supports a significant share of tribal household income in Purulia. Processing these in the village would keep the margin here.
  • ☀️Power from the plateau. Dry laterite uplands suit solar. The Ayodhya hills already host the Purulia Pumped Storage Project, operational since 2008.
