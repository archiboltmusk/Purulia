The Geographic Truth

The map has been read upside down.

Purulia is not peripheral. Dhanbad (~55 km), Jamshedpur (~60 km), Ranchi (~110 km) and Kolkata (~220 km) are all within 250 km as the crow flies, and Bhubaneswar about 340 km.

The problem has never been location. It has been the absence of infrastructure to activate it. That is a solvable problem — and this blueprint solves it.

  • ☀️Power from the plateau. Dry laterite uplands suit solar, and the Ayodhya hills already host the Purulia Pumped Storage Project. The 1,000 MW Turga pumped-storage project (₹6,922 crore, JICA loan) is planned in Bagmundi — and is before the Calcutta High Court over Forest Rights Act violations. Energy here has to be built with the forest villages, not over them.
  • 🎭World heritage, village incomes. Chhau dance is on UNESCO's list of intangible heritage, and Charida's mask-makers and the district's Dokra metalworkers are known far beyond Purulia. The infrastructure around them — workshops, design, market access — doesn't match the fame.
  • 🌿The forest is the economy. In one field study of 70 tribal households, firewood, sal leaves and sabai grass made up 47% of income. Mahua, sal and sabai leave the district raw; processing them in the village would keep the margin here.
  • 💧Drought is engineering, not fate. Purulia is drought-prone, yet it gets well over 1,000 mm of rain a year, most of it in the monsoon, and hard-rock ground stores little of it. The fix is storage — check dams, farm ponds, restored pukurs — block by block, with the new rural jobs guarantee paying for the work.
// 01 — Framework

Six Pillars.
One Transformation.

Each pillar is a standalone intervention. Together they form a flywheel. Sequence matters: power before roads, roads before industry, industry before talent retention.

Water & Geography

Harvest the monsoon. Store it. Check dams, farm ponds, watershed treatment convert a drought-prone plateau into a water-secure district. The fix is storage, not rainfall.

Politics
May 2026
2026 Assembly Election — A New Government, A Window to Act
The BJP won the 2026 West Bengal Assembly election, including Purulia, Raghunathpur, Bandwan, Joypur and Balarampur seats in the district. With the same party in the state and at the Centre, there is no longer an excuse for schemes stalled between the two. The test is whether stalled work — rural jobs, drinking water, Forest Rights Act titles — moves in the first year.
Science
Jan 2025
GSI Rare Earth Exploration — G2 Level
The Geological Survey of India has explored parts of Purulia for rare earths at the advanced G2 stage, under the National Critical Minerals Mission (Jan 2025). No block auctioned yet that we could find. Tribal safeguards and gram sabha consent must come before any auction.
Health
2024
Two Medical Colleges — PG Seat Push Underway
Purulia now has Deben Mahata Government Medical College and Bharat Medical College (NMC-approved, est. 2024). Next goal: NMC approval for PG seats and the first specialist arrivals that signal ecosystem viability.
