Helplines and your rights

Each number and step links to the government page it comes from. Only official sources are listed here.

Complain to the central government

CPGRAMS (pgportal.gov.in) takes grievances against central government departments.

  1. Open the portal and select Sign In to register or log in.
  2. Under Grievance, choose Lodge Public Grievance.
  3. Note the registration ID you receive, and use it to track the grievance.

The portal says grievances sent by email are not entertained. Use the portal, its mobile app, or UMANG.

Free legal aid

NALSA Helpline 15100, toll-free, from the National Legal Services Authority (nalsa.gov.in). The NALSA site lists it as its toll-free helpline.

Ask for information under the RTI Act

RTI Online (rtionline.gov.in) files requests with central ministries, departments and other central public authorities. The portal says applications for State Government bodies are returned without refund, so it does not cover them. Its helpline is 011-24010690 / 691, 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM, Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays (its email is listed on the site as helprtionline-dopt[at]nic[dot]in).

More numbers and West Bengal state services will be added once each one is confirmed on its official site. If you know a number that is missing, send it through Suggest a change with the official link.
