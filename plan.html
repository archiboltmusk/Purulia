The plan

Every item from What's next, with its status. Items are listed by the goal they serve. Nothing here has a date unless its source gives one. Ideas we rejected are listed separately.

Live on the site now · In progress being built · Waiting on an RTI request drafted or sent · Planned not started

Health: mothers and children

  • Live The Circle shows NFHS-5 figures for antenatal care, anaemia and child nutrition, and puts the district's maternal death ratio beside West Bengal and India.
  • Waiting on an RTI Maternal deaths for each year, by block and cause, asked of the CMOH by RTI. The request is drafted and still needs to be posted.
  • Live Report a health centre with no doctor, no medicines, or shut in working hours: a live photo at the gate, one tap to say which. In villages it goes in the weekly letter to the BDO.

Schools and girls' education

  • Live Schools compares Purulia with West Bengal and India using UDISE+ 2024-25, and anyone can check a school with a live photo or add one that is missing.
  • In progress An RTI draft asking the district for the UDISE+ export of every school in Purulia.
  • Planned Load each school's official record so every resident check sits beside what the government says, and a dropout figure for Purulia once that data arrives.

Work and migration

  • Live The Circle shows more marginal workers than main workers, and land that gives one crop a year (Census 2011, district profile).
  • Waiting on an RTI How many people leave for work: Karmasathi migrant registrations by block, asked of the Assistant Labour Commissioner by RTI. Drafted, still needs to be posted.
  • Live Rural jobs work checks: report an MGNREGA work that isn't there, or a worksite with no signboard, with a live photo at the spot, and compare it with the official work list for that gram panchayat.

Water

  • Live Only 32.45% of rural homes have a tap, the lowest in West Bengal (Jal Jeevan Mission). The water promise is tracked on Promises.
  • Live Report a tap that is fitted but gives no water, or a broken hand pump. In Purulia's villages it goes in a weekly letter to the Public Health Engineering office, so connections on paper can be set against taps that work.

Streets, drains and waste

  • Live The report map takes only live photos with real GPS, sets a deadline by how bad the problem is, and every ward has its own page.
  • Live A report closes only with a new live photo taken within 50 metres of the spot; a printable QR poster for each ward; a monthly view of the digest; and a weekly letter to each office listing its open reports. A report still open 14 days past its deadline also goes to the District Magistrate.

Naming who promised

  • Live Promises lists the MP and all nine MLAs with sourced promises, the Noticeboard puts public demands to named leaders, and The Circle names the Centre, state and district office for each step.
  • Live A dated verdict on each promise (in progress, delivered or broken), shown only with the document or photo that proves it, and every earlier verdict kept under it.
  • Planned Ask by RTI for the minutes of the MP-chaired DISHA committee, which reviews central schemes in the district, and publish them; none are online now.

A plan to stand on its own

  • Live The Blueprint starts from official figures (irrigation, tap water, forest, the pumped storage plant, two GI tags), and every plan in it is marked as our proposal.
  • Planned Recheck each Blueprint starting figure against its source once a year and show whether it moved.
  • Planned Ask for the days pending on every Purulia application to the state's Silpasathi single window, and publish them, so delays to new businesses are visible.

Keeping the site going

  • Live The report map works in English, Bengali, Hindi and Santali (Ol Chiki); moderators join by an invite link; a weekly encrypted backup of the database is set up and starts once its keys are added.
  • In progress Bengali and Hindi on The Circle, Schools, Noticeboard, Municipality and ward pages, and a page for volunteer groups.
  • Planned A second admin, so the site does not depend on one person, and ward drives with college NSS and NCC units as reporters and moderators.

Bengali and Hindi versions of this page are not on yet; the translated plan is on What's next. Something missing? Suggest it with a source.
