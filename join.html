// 06 — The First Five

Five People. Five Conversations.

Not a broadcast. Not a mass email. Five targeted messages this week. Each one moves one real thing. This is how blueprints become projects.

1
The District Collector
Or the MLA / MP with real local access
The only person who can guarantee a 3-year tenure and open the PIFC. Without political continuity, nothing compounds. This conversation starts everything else.
"Read the politicians section. Tell me what would need to be true for this to happen in your term."
2
The Solar Developer
A project head at a national renewable energy firm
Three signed solar developers create visible activity — cranes, construction, payments to farmers — within 18 months. This is the visible proof that changes the district's narrative before anything else does.
"We have pre-cleared land, 5.4 kWh/m² irradiance, and a single-window clearance centre opening in 6 months. Would your team take a site visit?"
3
The Purulia-Origin Senior
A director, VP, or partner — anywhere in India or abroad
One person with procurement power who is emotionally connected to Purulia can open supply chain conversations, make introductions, and invest — often all three, once they see the plan.
"Read the diaspora section. I want to know if you'd come to the first Pravasi Sammelan."
4
The Journalist
A state correspondent, development reporter, or regional editor
A single well-researched deep dive on local policy execution reaches the exact people who will never see this website. Media scrutiny creates administrative accountability and attracts investment simultaneously.
"I have a blueprint for transforming a Tier-4 district with ₹3,500 crore in central scheme money that's already available. The story is the gap between what's funded and what's executed."
5
The Doctor or Specialist
Ideally Purulia-origin, practicing in any Indian city
One specialist who publicly commits to setting up in Purulia — even tentatively — signals to the next specialist that Purulia is a real career option. Momentum is made of arrivals as much as announcements.
"Read the doctors section. Purulia now has two medical colleges and no specialist ecosystem around them yet. Would you consider building one?"
// 07 — The First Move

18 months.
One desk in the Collectorate.

These five actions unlock everything. Sequenced deliberately. Each creates conditions for the next. Nothing requires new money — only will and follow-through.

"The entire system depends on getting the first 3–5 anchor investors. Everything before is preparation. Everything after is momentum."

1
Digitise every land record in the district
Banglar Bhumi completion. Unblocks investment, infrastructure, acquisition. 12 months, no new money — only execution.
Start Now
2
Open the Purulia Investment Facilitation Centre
One office, one portal, all 12 departments. 30-day deemed approval. Signals to every investor that things have changed. Cost: ₹3–5 crore.
Month 1–6
3
Sign three anchor solar developers
Visible cranes. Land lease income for farmers within 18 months. Economic activity that creates its own momentum.
Month 3–12
4
Expand the medical colleges already here
Purulia has two medical colleges. Push NMC for PG seat approvals, recruit specialist faculty, and build the private practice ecosystem around them.
Month 1–3
5
Repair the five best government schools
Power, toilets, library, teacher monitoring. ₹15–25L per school. Signals to families that staying is viable. The most human ROI in the plan.
Month 1–6
2040
"Every district that breaks out of systemic poverty has a tipping point. It isn't magic; it is the moment when infrastructure aligns with local enterprise and people decide to stay...

Purulia's turning point story is not written yet.
That is not a problem.
That is the opportunity."

The solar farms going up on laterite land no one wanted. The Dokra artist getting her first order from Copenhagen. The engineer who came back from Bengaluru to start an O&M company. The child in a school in Bagmundi who will one day run the medical college. The architect designing a railway station that makes people feel something when they arrive.

Document all of it. In 2040, this becomes the Purulia Story — studied at LBSNAA, cited by the Finance Commission, and debated at every policy conference in India. That story starts with what happens in the next eighteen months.
