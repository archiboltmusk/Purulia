Hostel sanitation

Each floor has three daily checks: washroom, dustbin and corridor. The on-duty supervisor submits a check with a before and an after photo taken live with the camera, plus GPS. A floor representative then marks it verified or flagged. Public entries show only the facts, labelled Verified or Unverified, with date and source.

Problems found during a check can also be filed as reports on the map under “Hostel sanitation”.
