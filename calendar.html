Where each date comes from

Fix target: the day a report was filed plus 1, 3 or 7 days by how bad the reporter said it is (how it works). Warranty end: the completion date on a works board plus the defect liability period on the same board (public works). Promise deadline: the date a leader gave, from the source linked on promises. Swachh Pandal window: the puja days set for the Swachh Pandal list.
