01Water & GeographyYears 1–7↓
Phased Actions
  • Check dams on nalas, built through the rural jobs guarantee so wages stay in the village
  • Desilting abandoned pukurs — low-cost storage that villages already know how to use
  • Farm ponds under state water-harvesting schemes
  • Reforest catchments with sal, palash, mahua — improves soil permeability
  • Shift from paddy to bajra, ragi, pulses — drought-resilient alternatives
  • Drip and sprinkler irrigation for horticulture with government subsidy
  • Village Jal Shakti committees — maintenance ownership at GP level

02Economic Base — Three EnginesYears 1–12↓
Engine A — Solar Hub
  • Solar on barren laterite, with lease income for landowners (proposal; the size depends on a land and grid study nobody has published yet)
  • "Purulia Solar Zone" — a proposed fast-track environmental clearance designation
  • Solar module assembly park (proposal)
  • Green hydrogen pilot (Years 6–12) — a proposal, not a committed project
Engine B — Crafts Export
  • Use the GI tags Dokra and Purulia Chhau masks already hold — legal protection plus premium pricing
  • Artisan FPO collectives: own the brand, negotiate bulk orders globally
  • B2B export channels, institutional buyers, and premium D2C platforms
  • An experience centre: showroom + workshop + boutique stay (proposal)
Engine C — Agro-Processing
  • Mahua distillery cooperatives — tribal women as owners; craft spirits market is real
  • Sal seed cold-press: sal butter sells as a cocoa-butter substitute, so press it here instead of shipping seed out
  • Brand ragi + bajra as "Chota Nagpur Heritage Grains" — national health food market
  • 3–4 clusters with cold storage, FSSAI lab, packaging lines (proposal)
03Infrastructure — The SequencePower First↓
Power First — Non-Negotiable
  • Upgrade 33kV and 11kV substations in all block HQs; replace ageing transformers
  • Feeder separation: agriculture vs industrial
  • Publish feeder-level loss and outage data for the district, then set a public loss-reduction target
  • Captive solar microgrids for industrial clusters — a proposal for 24/7 power by Year 3
Roads
  • NH-32 complete 4-laning (proposal)
  • Varanasi–Ranchi–Kolkata expressway through the district (proposed)
  • Multimodal logistics park at NH-32 (proposal)
Broadband
  • BharatNet Phase 3 to every GP — execution, not funding, is the gap
  • Village Wi-Fi: local entrepreneur model, self-sustaining
  • 4 co-working centres — enables remote workers to choose to stay (proposal)
Who Pays
  • Power: RDSS, Centre 60% + State
  • Roads: NHAI / Bharatmala; rural roads PMGSY, Centre 60% + State 40%
  • Water: AMRUT 2.0 and Jal Jeevan Mission, Centre 50% + State
  • Broadband: BharatNet, Centre 100%
04Stopping Brain DrainThe Hardest Problem↓
Anchor Institutions
  • Medical colleges (existing — expand them): Purulia has Deben Mahata Government Medical College. The goal is PG seat expansion, specialist retention, and making the institution an anchor employer.
  • NIT satellite (proposal): Renewable energy engineering focus.
  • Craft University (long-term proposal): A craft-and-design school rooted in Chhau and Dokra, working with the artisans.
Purulia Roots — Return Migration
  • Map Purulia-origin professionals globally — LinkedIn, GP surveys, NRI networks
  • Annual Pravasi Sammelan: investment pitches, startup matching, land allocation
  • Proposed land allocations and tax incentives for diaspora enterprises
  • Young Leaders Fellowship: a proposal for paid positions on district development

05Investment Climate ReformMake the Single Window Work↓
Three Proposed Industrial Zones
  • Solar & Green Energy Park (Hura/Kashipur): Pre-laid 33kV feeder, fibre, water.
  • Tribal Crafts Cluster (near Purulia town): Shared kilns, looms, casting furnaces. APEDA + customs on-site. Artisan-owned IP.
  • Agro-Processing Park (Manbazar): Cold storage and an FSSAI lab.
Clearances — Silpasathi, Locally
  • Use the state's Silpasathi single window; a district desk to help Purulia applicants through it
  • Publish every Purulia application and its days pending — delays become visible
  • Relationship manager for every investment above ₹5 crore (proposal)
  • Anti-corruption: mystery shopping, face-less processing, officer rotation (proposal)

06Connectivity & PositioningRail · Road · Air · Digital↓
Rail
  • A faster Howrah–Purulia intercity (proposal) — a Vande Bharat or dedicated service would make day trips from Kolkata practical
  • Adra–Ranchi route: push for doubling and faster trains (proposal)
  • Better rail links south towards Odisha's ports (proposal)
  • Station redevelopment: craft market, Chhau art, co-working (proposal)
Air + Digital
  • Study regional flights under UDAN (proposal; cost unknown until a study is done)
  • Possible routes: Purulia–Kolkata and Purulia–Ranchi
  • BharatNet, then GP Wi-Fi, then mobile data along NH-32 (proposal)
  • Brand: "Eastern India's Green Economy Frontier"
07Accountability SystemsPressure on Systems, Not People↓
Publish the Rankings
  • The PMO's PRAYAS platform tracks schemes and indicators across departments. Ask for Purulia's scheme-by-scheme rank among West Bengal districts to be published on purulia.gov.in
  • The MP-chaired DISHA committee reviews central schemes in the district — put the rankings on its agenda and publish the minutes
  • Laggard alert (proposal): any scheme where Purulia sits below the state median for two months running gets a public note
Assets Anyone Can Check
  • Rural-jobs assets are geotagged before, during, and after construction (GeoMGNREGA on ISRO's Bhuvan). Keep this under the new VB–G RAM G Act
  • Put a QR code on every work's signboard, linking to its geotagged record
  • If it's broken or missing, report it on Parishkar Bengal from the spot: a live photo and GPS, public, and only closed when neighbours confirm the fix
Clearance Clock
  • Every Purulia application on the state's Silpasathi single window, with its days pending, published
Channels That Must Reply
  • Chief Minister's helpline "Apnar Sarkar Apnar Pashe": 82820 82820 (Mon–Sat, 9–6) or asap@wb.gov.in
  • Central grievance portal CPGRAMS (pgportal.gov.in): replies due within 21 days
  • RTI online: ask for the work order, budget, and completion certificate
  • Parishkar Bengal links all of these from every report

// 04 — The Roadmap

2026 to 2040.

Four phases. Each builds conditions for the next. The timeline below is a proposal — not a committed plan.

Phase 01 · 2026–2028
The Foundation Sprint
  • Clear pending land-record and forest-rights cases, with the list published
  • Publish a Purulia tracker on the Silpasathi single window
  • Sign first solar developers
  • Expand PG seats at Deben Mahata GMC
  • Repair top schools per block
  • Map Purulia-origin professionals
  • First Young Leaders Fellowship cohort
→ Foundation laid, first investors visible
Phase 02 · 2028–2031
First Engines Running
  • Solar capacity online
  • Dokra + Chhau GI tags used for premium sales
  • Agro-processing cluster operational
  • BharatNet to every GP
  • Faster Howrah–Purulia train service
  • Regional flights, if a study supports them
  • Annual Pravasi Sammelan
→ Economic activity visible, narrative shifts
Phase 03 · 2031–2036
The Tipping Point
  • Medical colleges fully staffed, PG programmes running
  • Solar park operational
  • Fewer families leave for seasonal work (goal; to be checked against the next Census)
  • Varanasi–Ranchi–Kolkata expressway open through the district
  • Craft and forest exports growing
  • Skilled workers thriving locally
  • National press interest
→ Staying becomes a real choice
Phase 04 · 2036–2040
The Flywheel Era
  • NIT satellite campus open
  • Solar capacity growing, green hydrogen pilot live
  • People choosing to come back (goal)
  • Freight spur to Kolkata port (proposal)
  • District model shared with other states
  • Momentum owns itself
→ Purulia becomes a proof of concept for India
