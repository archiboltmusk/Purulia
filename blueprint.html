02Economic Base — Three EnginesYears 1–12↓
Engine A — Solar Hub
  • 500–1,000MW installed; land leasing at ₹25–40k/acre/year on barren laterite
  • "Purulia Solar Zone" — fast-track environmental clearance designation
  • Solar module assembly park: 500MW/year capacity, 2,000+ direct jobs
  • Green hydrogen pilot (Yrs 6–12): excess solar → Kolkata industrial belt
Engine B — Crafts Export
  • GI tags for Dokra metalwork and Chhau masks — legal protection + premium pricing
  • Artisan FPO collectives: own the brand, negotiate bulk orders globally
  • B2B export channels, institutional buyers, and premium D2C platforms — artisan income target ₹20–25k/month
  • Flagship experience centre: showroom + workshop + boutique stay
Engine C — Agro-Processing
  • Mahua distillery cooperatives — tribal women as owners; craft spirits market is real
  • Sal seed cold-press: ₹40/kg raw → ₹200–400/kg refined (cocoa butter equivalent)
  • Brand ragi + bajra as "Chota Nagpur Heritage Grains" — national health food market
  • 3–4 clusters with cold storage, FSSAI lab, packaging lines

03Infrastructure — The Sequence₹3,500 Crore↓
Power First — Non-Negotiable
  • Upgrade 33kV and 11kV substations in all block HQs; replace 20–30yr old transformers
  • Feeder separation: agriculture vs industrial — Gujarat did this in 2003, transformed manufacturing
  • Target <2% transmission loss (currently ~18%)

Roads

Broadband

  • Village Wi-Fi: local entrepreneur model, ₹10–20/day, self-sustaining
  • 4 co-working centres — enables remote workers to choose to stay

04Stopping Brain DrainThe Hardest Problem↓
The Anchor Institution
  • Medical colleges (existing — expand them): Purulia already has Deben Mahata Government Medical College (100 MBBS seats) and Bharat Medical College (150 MBBS seats, est. 2024). The goal is now PG seat expansion, specialist retention, and making these institutions anchor employers.
  • NIT satellite (second): Renewable energy engineering focus. 1,000 students = permanent young educated population building careers locally.
  • Craft University (third, 20yr): India has no institution for traditional craft + contemporary design. A Purulia NID satellite is globally unique.
Purulia Roots — Return Migration
  • Map 5,000 Purulia-origin professionals globally — LinkedIn, GP surveys, NRI networks
  • Annual Pravasi Sammelan: investment pitches, startup matching, land allocation
  • Land at subsidised rates + 5-year tax holiday for diaspora enterprises
  • Young Leaders Fellowship: 20 people/year, 2-year paid positions on district development

05Investment Climate Reform37 → 1 Clearance↓
Three Industrial Zones
  • Solar & Green Energy Park (Hura/Kashipur): Pre-laid 33kV feeder, fibre, water. Land at ₹5L/acre. 5yr GST reimbursement. Target: 25 units, 8,000 jobs, ₹3,000Cr.
  • Tribal Crafts Cluster (near Purulia town): Shared kilns, looms, casting furnaces. APEDA + customs on-site. Artisan-owned IP — no corporate extraction.
  • Agro-Processing Park (Manbazar): 5,000MT cold storage, FSSAI lab. ₹500Cr annual revenue target by 2032.
PIFC — Single Window
  • One portal: submit once, all 12 departments notified simultaneously
  • 30-day deemed approval: no response = clearance granted automatically
  • Relationship manager for every investment above ₹5 crore
  • Anti-corruption: mystery shopping, face-less processing, officer rotation

// 04 — The Roadmap

2026 to 2040.

Four phases. Each builds conditions for the next. The flywheel starts when visible results create self-sustaining momentum.

Phase 01 · 2026–2028
The Foundation Sprint
  • Digitise all land records
  • Launch PIFC single-window clearance
  • Sign first 3 solar developers
  • Expand PG seats at Deben Mahata GMC
  • Repair top schools per block
  • Map 5,000 diaspora professionals
  • First Young Leaders Fellowship
→ Foundation laid, first investors visible
Phase 02 · 2028–2031
First Engines Running
  • 500MW solar capacity online
  • Dokra + Chhau GI tags secured
  • Agro-processing cluster operational
  • BharatNet to every GP
  • Fast Howrah–Purulia express (3.5hr target)
  • Regional airport open (UDAN)
  • Annual Pravasi Sammelan
→ Economic activity visible, narrative shifts
Phase 03 · 2031–2036
The Tipping Point
  • Medical colleges fully staffed, PG programmes running
  • Solar park: 25 units, 8,000 jobs
  • Net migration slows — first time
  • Purulia–Ranchi expressway open
  • ₹500Cr/yr craft + forest exports
  • 200 high-calibre people thriving locally
  • National press: "the comeback city"
→ Net migration begins reversing
Phase 04 · 2036–2040
The Flywheel Era
  • NIT satellite campus open
  • 1,000MW+ solar, green hydrogen live
  • Net in-migration — people choosing here
  • DFC spur to Kolkata port
  • LBSNAA curriculum includes the Purulia model
  • NITI Aayog adopts the district framework
  • Momentum owns itself
→ Purulia is a proof of concept for India
