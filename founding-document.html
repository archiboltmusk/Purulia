PARISHKAR PURULIA

Memorandum of Association of a Registered Society

(Under the West Bengal Societies Registration Act, 1961)

1. Name of the Society

The name of the Society shall be PARISHKAR PURULIA, in Bengali পরিষ্কার পুরুলিয়া, in Hindi परिष्कार पुरुलिया.

2. Registered Office

The registered office of the Society shall be at: ________________________________________, Purulia, West Bengal — 723101.

3. Aims and Objectives

The Society is established for charitable, educational, and civic purposes. Its objectives are:

  • To create and maintain a public, verifiable record of civic problems in Purulia district — including but not limited to sanitation, roads, drainage, street lighting, water supply, education infrastructure, public health facilities, and environmental concerns.
  • To enable residents of Purulia to document civic problems with photographic evidence and verified location data.
  • To track the response of the responsible authorities — municipality, district administration, and line departments — to publicly documented civic problems.
  • To ensure that no problem is marked resolved without independent, on-the-spot verification by multiple citizens.
  • To publish open data on civic problems and their resolution status, freely accessible to citizens, journalists, researchers, and government bodies.
  • To promote civic awareness, environmental responsibility, and public participation in local governance.
  • To carry out any other activity that is incidental or conducive to the above objectives.

4. Vision

Purulia district has real, documented civic infrastructure gaps in sanitation, water, roads, health and education access. The Society exists to make the state of that infrastructure a matter of public, verifiable record, rather than a matter of unverifiable claim by any single party — citizen, official, or otherwise.

5. Methods of Work

The Society shall achieve its objectives through:

  • A publicly accessible web platform at ____________________ (the "Platform") where citizens can file reports of civic problems.
  • Verification of cleanup or resolution claims through on-site photographic evidence submitted by multiple independent citizens.
  • Publication of open data in machine-readable formats under a Creative Commons license.
  • Engagement with media, civil society organisations, and government bodies to make the public record known and used.
  • Any other lawful means consistent with the objectives.

6. Governance

The Society shall be managed by a Governing Body of not fewer than 7 (seven) and not more than 15 (fifteen) members. The Governing Body shall meet at least once every quarter and shall maintain minutes of all meetings.

The Governing Body shall elect, from among its members:

  • A President
  • A Secretary
  • A Treasurer

The term of office of the Governing Body shall be 3 (three) years, and members shall be eligible for re-election.

7. Membership

Any person of sound mind, above 18 years of age, who subscribes to the objectives of the Society, may apply for membership. Membership is not restricted by caste, creed, religion, gender, language, or political affiliation.

Membership shall not be denied on any basis other than non-subscription to the objectives of the Society or prior conviction for a crime involving moral turpitude.

8. Funds and Accounts

The Society may receive funds through:

  • Voluntary donations from individuals, trusts, and corporations
  • Grants from government bodies
  • Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) contributions
  • Fellowships, prizes, and awards

All funds shall be deposited in a bank account in the name of the Society. The Governing Body shall maintain proper books of accounts, and the accounts shall be audited annually by a qualified Chartered Accountant.

No part of the funds or income of the Society shall be distributed as profit, dividend, or bonus to any member.

9. Non-Political Character

The Society shall be strictly non-political. It shall not be affiliated with, or act on behalf of, any political party, political organisation, or elected representative. Its work is civic, independent, and evidence-based.

10. Dissolution

If the Society is ever dissolved, all its assets, after settling debts and liabilities, shall be transferred to another registered society, trust, or non-profit company with similar objectives. No asset shall be distributed to any member.

11. Amendments

This Memorandum may be amended by a resolution passed by two-thirds of the members present at a General Meeting, subject to the provisions of the West Bengal Societies Registration Act, 1961.
