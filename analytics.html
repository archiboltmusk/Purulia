Letters to the offices responsible

Every Monday each office with an open report in its area gets a letter listing them, with photo and location: the municipality for town wards, the BDO for village blocks. A report still open 14 days past its deadline also goes to the District Magistrate. Office emails: District Disaster Management Plan Purulia 2023-24 and purulia.gov.in.

What moderators and automatic checks did

Every moderator action and automatic check is counted here, month by month. Reasons are shown on each report's own evidence trail.

Forests from space

Parishkar doesn't track tree loss itself. Global Forest Watch publishes satellite alerts where tree cover disappears, updated every few days. The map below opens on Purulia: turn on Integrated deforestation alerts. An alert is a clue, not proof: it can be a harvest or a fire. If you can see illegal felling or mining on the spot, report it with a photo.
