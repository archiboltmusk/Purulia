Swachh Pandal

Puja crowds leave plates, cups and plastic behind. This page lists the pandals people have added and counts the photo reports filed within 100 m of each one during the puja: how many were cleaned up, and how many are still waiting. Nothing else goes into the order, so a pandal moves up only when its surroundings stay clean or get cleaned.

How it works

1. Add the pandalStand at the pandal and open Add a pandal. Give its name and the club or committee.
2. Report what you seePlates, cups or plastic piling up near a pandal? Report it with a live photo, like any other report. It counts for the nearest pandal.
3. Clean it, prove itThe club, volunteers or the municipality clean it and add an after photo. The report moves from open to cleaned up.
4. Share the listClubs that keep their para clean rise to the top. Share the page with your puja committee.
