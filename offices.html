Who owns the open reports

Open reports from the public record, grouped by the office that owns them. Counts and dates only. Owners are set by the rules at the bottom of this page; an office with no contact on file says so.

Loading…

Owner rules: a town ward goes to the municipality. A village dry tap or broken hand pump goes to PHE (Civil). A road goes to PWD, National highway or Zilla Parishad by its OpenStreetMap road tag (IN:SH, IN:NH, IN:MDR/ODR/VR). Any other village report goes to the block BDO. Contacts and their sources: DDMP Purulia 2023-24 (pp.34, 198, 199). State grievance portal: not verified yet.
