How to adopt a spot

1. Stand thereGo to the spot you will look after: your shop front, school gate, the corner by your club.
2. Give a public nameOpen Adopt a spot and type the name people know you by. Your account stays private.
3. Keep it cleanAny report within 50 m shows against your spot until it is fixed. One person can look after up to 3 spots.
