India · proposed pilots

Health projects that start with people

Nine concepts for making primary care more reachable. They are proposals, not deployed clinical services or medical advice.

Safety before scale. Every pilot requires clinical governance, informed consent, data minimisation, security review, accessibility testing, and a responsible public-health partner. A project is not launched merely because it is listed here.
01

Elderly care at home

A consent-based check-in and care-coordination pilot for older people living alone, designed with families, nurses, and local health workers.

02

Home HPV test kit

A referral and follow-up pathway for validated self-sampling programmes—never a substitute for diagnosis, counselling, or clinical confirmation.

03

Village TB testing

A field workflow to help accredited teams route screening and samples to approved laboratories, with clear referral and result follow-up.

04

Made-in-India medical devices

A transparent needs register connecting frontline repair, usability, affordability, and procurement evidence to regulated device development.

05

WhatsApp assistant for ASHAs

A worker-controlled reminder and referral helper, designed to reduce paperwork without exposing family records or replacing professional judgement.

06

Rapid antibiotic testing

A clinical decision-support pilot for approved diagnostics and stewardship workflows; results remain under qualified clinician oversight.

07

Home stroke rehabilitation

An accessible exercise and follow-up pathway co-designed with rehabilitation clinicians, caregivers, and people recovering from stroke.

08

Home BP & diabetes monitoring

A patient-owned log and escalation pathway that helps care teams review readings without claiming to diagnose or replace urgent care.

09

Clinic digital records

A lightweight, consent-based clinic record system built for continuity of care, interoperable export, and strict role-based access.

Work with a local public-health institution, ethics review, and accountable clinical lead before any pilot.
