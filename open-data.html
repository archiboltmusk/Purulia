Open data

Everything on the map is built from the files below. Each one names its source; take it, check it against the source, and reuse it on the source's own terms. Reports are residents' allegations with a live photo and GPS, not verified facts.

Reports: Open311 feed

The public reports are available read-only in the Open311 GeoReport v2 format, so other civic apps, newsrooms and offices can pull them without scraping. JSON only. New reports can't be sent this way: a report needs a live camera photo and real GPS, which only the report map checks.

  • Problem types: /open311/v2/services.json
  • Reports: /open311/v2/requests.json, with start_date, end_date, status=open|closed, service_code (comma-separated). Without dates: the last 90 days, up to 1000.
  • One report: /open311/v2/requests/<id>.json

The same numbers as tables, one CSV per table, are on the numbers page.
