The record: harms, evidence, remedies

Each record here is one public problem, traced document by document: what the official record says (with page numbers), what contradicts it, which complaint or case it is in, what was asked to fix it, and whether the fix was checked on the ground. Nothing here is an accusation: an FIR is not guilt, an audit remark is not a finding, and a closed complaint is not a fixed problem. How records are checked.
