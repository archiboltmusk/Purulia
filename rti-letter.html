RTI letter

Type your details; the letter builds on this page. Nothing is sent, stored or filed.

Fee: ₹10 is the central rate (RTI Rules, 2012). States may set a different fee or method. Check your state's RTI rules before you pay. If you are below the poverty line, strike out the fee line and attach an attested BPL ration card under s.7(5).

Your letter

Sending it

  • Fee: ₹10 Indian Postal Order payable to the Accounts Officer, Office of the District Magistrate, or a ₹10 court fee stamp on page 1. State practice may differ.
  • Send by Speed Post or Registered Post AD, or hand it in at the District Collectorate's RTI cell and get a stamped acknowledgement with a diary number.
  • The Public Information Officer must reply within 30 days (s.7(1)). If there is no reply, note the first-appeal date in the RTI tracker.
