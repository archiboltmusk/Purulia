Escalate an unresolved report

Use this when a report is still open after its fix date. Each step names its source. Parishkar does not file anything for you.

Before you escalate

  • Open the report's case file and note the days open and the fix date (set by the report's own timeline, shown there).
  • Note the office that answers for the spot ("who answers" on the case file).
  • Keep a copy of every letter, application and reference number. Record each one on the report under Filed officially.

The steps, in order

StepWho to write toTimelineSource
1. Ask the office that owns the spot The office shown as answering for the spot on the case file (municipality, panchayat, block or district office). Fix date on the case file. After it passes, the report is overdue. Parishkar's own fix-date rule, shown on each case file.
2. Write to the elected representative The ward councillor, MLA or MP for the spot (leader profile on Parishkar, from the official constituency record). No statutory deadline. Log the reply under "Reply from this leader" so it is public, unedited. Leader profile on Parishkar: MLA/MP from Wikipedia constituency results (places/wb_leaders.json).
3. File an RTI application The Public Information Officer (PIO) of that office. Ask for the file, the work order, the inspection record and the fix date. Reply due within 30 days of receipt. Within 48 hours if life or liberty is at stake. RTI Act 2005, s.7(1).
4. First appeal The first appellate authority named in the PIO's reply, or in the office's RTI notice. Within 30 days after the reply was due or received. With no reply, that is 60 days from filing. RTI Act 2005, s.19(1).
5. Second appeal The State Information Commission (for West Bengal state bodies). Within 90 days of the decision, or of the date it was due. RTI Act 2005, s.19(3).
6. Central department grievance CPGRAMS, only for central government departments. It does not cover state or local bodies. Track with the registration ID the portal gives you. Helplines and your rights; pgportal.gov.in.
7. Free legal aid NALSA legal services, toll-free. Call 15100 when a legal step is needed. nalsa.gov.in; Helplines.

Dates to watch

RTI dates run from the filing date (step 3) and the reply date (step 4). Record each RTI with its filing date and Parishkar works out the reply due date (+30 days) and the first-appeal date (+60 days with no reply). Confirm the dates against the RTI Act before you rely on them.

Writing the RTI application

  • Name the office, the spot, and the date the report was made. Attach the case-file link.
  • Ask for documents that already exist. An RTI cannot ask an office to answer "why".
  • Keep the request to one subject. Use the same wording in any later appeal.

Record and share

Every step you file can go on the report under Filed officially, with its reference number. Anyone can then see what was asked and when. The reply, if one comes, can be posted unedited under the same report.

Only steps with an official source are listed. Find the state grievance portal and the State Information Commission link on their official sites before adding them here. If you know one that is missing, send it through Suggest a change with the official link.
