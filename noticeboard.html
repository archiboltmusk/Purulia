    What belongs here

    • Ask for something the whole ward, village or town needs: a road, streetlights, a drain, water, a school teacher, a bus stop.
    • Not for yourself or your family. For your own pension, certificate, house or job, use the Grievance page or the municipality helpline.
    • Name the leader and say why it helps everyone. No insults, no party slogans, no names of private people. A volunteer moderator checks every demand before it appears.

    Add a leader's reply

    Did the leader answer this in a speech, a letter, a newspaper or an official post? Add what they said and a link to it. A moderator checks the link first.

    Post a demand

    Keep it short and specific: what is needed, where, and who it helps. It appears here once a moderator has checked it.
