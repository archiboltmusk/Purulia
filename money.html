Tax and spending: where the money goes

How much tax a state collects itself, how much of the budget its Assembly passed was actually spent, and what the government's auditor, the Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG), flagged. Every figure links to the page it comes from. No official report gives one number for "money wasted", so this page shows what the auditor does report: money left unspent, grants with no proof of what they bought, and spending beyond what was approved.
