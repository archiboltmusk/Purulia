Rural road tenders

Source: PMGSY eProcurement, page header dated 16 Oct 2026. The list changes, so check the site for the latest.

The PMGSY eProcurement site lists active rural-road tenders with their closing dates. On the list when checked, tenders close from 21 Oct 2026 to 28 Oct 2026. Corrigenda and date extensions are listed there too.

The total number of tenders was not counted, so none is given here. To see a tender, open the list on the site and search by state or district.
