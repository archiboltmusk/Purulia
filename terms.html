1. What Purulia Kasa Is

Purulia Kasa ("the Platform", "we", "us") is a free, non-commercial civic reporting tool that allows residents of Purulia town, West Bengal, to publicly report civic problems in their neighbourhood — garbage and drains, roads and streetlights, water supply, damaged public property, encroachment and other suspected illegal activity — and to follow whether they are fixed.

The Platform is intended for public interest civic engagement. It is not a political campaign, not affiliated with any political party, and not a formal grievance-redressal mechanism of any government body.

You must be 18 or older to use the Platform, or use it with the consent of a parent or guardian.

2. Nature of Content — Reports Are Allegations, Not Facts

Every report published on Purulia Kasa is a citizen allegation. It reflects the personal observation and opinion of the individual who submitted it. It is not a verified fact, not an official finding, and not a legal determination of any kind.

The Platform does not independently verify the accuracy, completeness, or truthfulness of any report. Users are solely responsible for the content they publish.

Labels such as "Resolved", "Verified by neighbours" or "Neighbours doubt this" record what other users confirmed or rated at the location. They are not official findings either, and they do not establish that anyone was or was not negligent.

By using this Platform, you acknowledge and agree that:

  • Reports describe what a citizen claims to have seen at a specific time and place.
  • No report should be read as an accusation of criminal conduct by any named or identifiable person.
  • Councillors, officials, and elected representatives named on this Platform are named because of the office they hold, and are presumed innocent of any negligence or misconduct.
  • Readers must draw their own conclusions and are encouraged to verify facts independently.

3. User Responsibilities

If you submit a report, confirmation, dispute, rating or flag, you agree that:

  • You are solely responsible for the accuracy of the information and the legality of the photograph you upload.
  • Your submission is truthful to the best of your knowledge. You will not submit anything knowingly false, fabricated, exaggerated, or intended to harass any individual — including false cleanup claims, confirmations or disputes.
  • You will not upload photographs that contain identifiable people without their consent, minors, vehicle number plates where avoidable, obscene or violent imagery, or content unrelated to the stated civic issue.
  • You will not confront, follow or endanger anyone in order to report. In an emergency, call 112.
  • You will not use the Platform for defamation, political campaigning, commercial advertising, or any unlawful purpose.
  • You will not submit content that infringes any third party's copyright, privacy, or other legal rights.
  • You will not offer, pay, accept or arrange money, goods or favours for a cleanup claim, confirmation, dispute, rating or flag, and you will not ask other people to confirm or dispute something they have not checked themselves on the spot. Arranging false confirmations may be a criminal offence, for example criminal conspiracy under Section 61 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023. Claims and votes that appear to be arranged are thrown out, and the accounts involved can be blocked. We will share the limited data we hold (see the Privacy Policy) with the police only when the law requires it.
  • The text, location, category and status history of your report are published as open data under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0) licence, so anyone may reuse them with credit to “Purulia Kasa”. Photos are not covered by this licence.

The following content is strictly prohibited and will be removed without notice:

  • Content that is defamatory, libellous, or seditious.
  • Content that promotes hatred, violence, or discrimination based on religion, caste, gender, or political affiliation.
  • Content that is obscene, pornographic, paedophilic, or harmful to minors.

Reports of encroachment, illegal construction, illegal mining and other illegal activity are reviewed by a moderator before they are shown publicly.

5. Platform Neutrality & Safe Harbour

Purulia Kasa operates as an intermediary under Section 2(1)(w) of the Information Technology Act, 2000. We do not initiate the transmission of user reports, do not select the recipients, and do not modify the content uploaded by users (other than resizing photographs for faster loading).

We claim protection under Section 79 of the IT Act, 2000, which provides safe harbour to intermediaries for content posted by users, provided that:

  • We observe due diligence as required under the IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021.
  • We do not knowingly host or publish unlawful content.
  • We act expeditiously upon receiving actual knowledge of any unlawful content.

6. Grievance Officer & Content Removal

In accordance with Rule 3(2) of the IT (Intermediary Guidelines) Rules, 2021, we have designated a Grievance Officer:

  • Name: Anupam Mahato (Administrator)
  • Email: grievance@puruliakasa.in
  • Response time: Acknowledgement within 24 hours; resolution within 15 days.

If you believe any content on the Platform is unlawful, defamatory, or violates your rights, please email the Grievance Officer with:

  • The specific URL of the report.
  • A description of the harm.
  • Your identity and contact information.
  • A statement of good faith and accuracy.

We will review the complaint and take appropriate action, which may include removal of the content without prior notice. Content that exposes a person's private areas or impersonates them is removed within 24 hours of a complaint. Content that a court order or an authorised government notice requires us to remove is removed within 36 hours. Removed content and its records are preserved privately for 180 days, as the Rules require, and are not shown publicly. See the Grievance Officer page for details and appeals.

7. Right of Reply

Any councillor, municipal official, or elected representative named or identifiable in a report has the right to submit a formal response. Responses are published on the relevant report's detail view alongside the original report, labelled "Official response", without edits other than removing unlawful content. A response does not change a report's status; only confirmations made at the location do.

To submit a response, email the Grievance Officer with your name, position, the report link and your statement — preferably from an official address so we can confirm who you are. The report page also has a "Send a response" link that prepares this email.
