Ideas we rejected

Each idea below was considered and not built. The reason is one line: privacy, cost, or not a fit. Ideas still planned are on The plan.
  • Blockchain or any public ledger. Not a fit. A ledger cannot take a record back. Reports, photos and corrections have to be removable and fixable.
  • Torrent or peer-to-peer swarms for reports and photos. Privacy. Copies would sit on strangers' devices, where no one can correct or remove them.
  • Public Git, IPFS or other permanent mirrors of all reports. Privacy. Once copied, a report cannot be pulled back, including reports that were later hidden or corrected.
  • Sensor tracking: motion logs, Wi-Fi or Bluetooth scans, cell-tower lookups. Privacy. A report needs one live photo and the real GPS at the spot. Nothing else is collected.
  • Unvetted crowdsourcing of official figures or promises. Not a fit. Every figure and promise needs a cited source, and a moderator checks it before it goes live.
  • A made-up 'truth score' or percentage for any claim. Not a fit. A number with no method would look like a finding. Figures are shown with their source instead.
  • Auto-posting alerts to X or Telegram. Not a fit. The owner chose not to. Alerts go by email, the bell on the report map and the weekly digest.

Bengali and Hindi versions of this page are not on yet. Something you would add to this list? Suggest it with a reason.
