    Add a promise, or an update

    Heard a promise in a speech, a newspaper or an official post? Add it with a link to where it was said. Seen a promise delivered, started or dropped? Use "Suggest an update" on that promise and add a link that shows it. A moderator checks every link before anything appears here.

    How this page stays fair

    • Every promise links to where it was said, with the date. No link, no entry.
    • "In progress", "delivered" and "broken" each need their own link and date. Without one, a promise stays at "nothing shown yet".
    • A volunteer moderator checks each link before it is published. News headlines are collected by a free daily job and are only links, never a verdict.
    • Anyone named here can reply or correct an entry through the Grievance Officer. For who controls the municipality and its money, see Who runs the municipality.
