Grievance Officer

Designated under Rule 3(2) of IT (Intermediary Guidelines) Rules, 2021

Parishkar Purulia operates as an intermediary under Section 2(1)(w) of the Information Technology Act, 2000. We have designated a Grievance Officer in accordance with Rule 3(2) of the IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021.

What you can raise

  • Unlawful or defamatory content — a report, photo, description or response you believe is false, defamatory or illegal.
  • Privacy — a photo that shows your face, home, vehicle number plate or other personal details.
  • Right of reply — if you are a councillor, official or elected representative named or identifiable in a report, you can send a formal response to be published alongside it.
  • Your own data — requests to delete or correct a report you filed, or to know what we hold (see the Privacy Policy).
  • Legal orders — court orders and notices from an authorised government agency.

How to make a complaint

Email the Grievance Officer with:

  1. The link to the report (open the report and use "Share" or "Copy report link").
  2. What is wrong, and the harm it causes you.
  3. Your name and contact details, and — for a right of reply — your position.
  4. A statement that the information in your complaint is accurate and made in good faith.

If you are an official sending a response, please write from an official address, or tell us how we can confirm your identity. Responses are published on the report, labelled "Official response", without edits other than removing unlawful content.

What happens next

SituationWhat we do
Any complaintAcknowledge within 24 hours; decide and reply within 15 days.
Content that exposes a person's private areas, shows them in full or partial nudity or a sexual act, or impersonates themRemoved within 24 hours of the complaint.
A court order, or a notice from an authorised government agencyRemoved within 36 hours of receipt.
Content we removeHidden from the public immediately; preserved privately for 180 days as the Rules require, in case it is needed for an investigation.

Removing content does not require prior notice to the person who posted it. Reports are never deleted or marked "resolved" because of a complaint alone. If a complaint is about whether a problem was fixed, the fix is decided by people confirming it at the location.

If you are not satisfied

You can appeal the Grievance Officer's decision to the Grievance Appellate Committee set up by the Government of India under Rule 3A of the IT Rules, within 30 days of receiving the decision, at gac.gov.in. Complaints about personal data can also be taken to the Data Protection Board of India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
