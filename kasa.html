03 · Verification

Why “resolved” here means resolved

No official or party worker can close a report by saying so. Neighbours on the spot confirm a fix. An admin can accept a cleanup only after checking the photos, and the report then says so openly. These rules run on the server — the website can't skip them.

    Look after this spot

    Your shop, school, club or family keeps the 50 m around where you are standing clean. The name goes on the public list with how the spot is doing. A shop and a club can look after the same spot together.

    Only the name you type is public, never your account. You can let the spot go any time from the Adopted spots page.

    Register a dog feeding spot

    Stand where you feed the dogs. Pick a spot away from school gates, play areas, entrances and stairs. A moderator checks it before it goes on the public list.

    Only the name you type is public, never your account. You promise to clean up after every feed. You can stop any time from the Community dogs page.
