// 04 — How It Works

Four steps. Thirty seconds.

01
Snap a photo
Take a picture of the garbage, right where it is. No account, no sign-up.
02
Pin the spot
GPS captures the exact coordinates. Confirm your ward from the dropdown.
03
It goes public
Your report appears instantly on the live map and the ward leaderboard.
04
We escalate it
One tap sends your report card straight to Purulia Municipality on WhatsApp or email.
